Dung Beetle Carnival Party

by UnCommonSoft

🎶 This is a "flavorful" adventure and carnival! Transform into a hardworking (and persistent) dung beetle in the microscopic world, immersing yourself in simulating the life of a dung beetle. Use your pincers to grip the ground and crawl, keep pushing dung balls with all your strength using your forelegs, or grab dung balls to hurl them as weapons at enemies! Crawling, grabbing, throwing—truly "pushing dung balls with your own hands"!

From mud pits to flower clusters, keep rolling to make the dung balls bigger. There's only one goal: fetch fresh cow dung and horse dung from far away, and host the grandest dung ball party on the grassland! But the journey won't be easy—sneaky flies will try to steal your dung, grumpy bees will come to cause trouble, and ants and pill bugs will occasionally "help" (or hinder) you. You'll constantly engage in fun offensive and defensive battles with these interesting insects.

As a beetle, you can climb anywhere: mushrooms, stumps, mounds, even your friends! You can also switch to your desired dung beetle species skins, showing off your shiny, handsome carapaces in various colors to your pals!

Crawl into tree holes, light up mushrooms as lamps, host multiplayer dung ball parties, and unlock different mysterious endings through diverse gameplay.

🎉 Roll on, warrior! Your dung ball kingdom awaits you! 💩

🌿 Come on, let's join this "flavorful" grassland ecological adventure together and unlock the joy of the carnival party! 🔥
